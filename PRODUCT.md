## Positioning provenance

**No discovery call has happened.** Every positioning claim in this file is
inferred from public reviews (Google, Yelp, Nextdoor; 4.8 stars across 67
Google reviews at time of writing), not from the practice. Nothing here is
client-confirmed. Treat each claim as a hypothesis Dr. Staehling must verify
before launch. Flagged placeholders in the copy contracts are never silently
resolved with plausible inventions.

## Users

Three profiles, in priority order:

1. **Anxious returners.** Adults with dental anxiety, often returning after
   years of avoidance. They arrive braced for judgment and pressure. Their
   job: find a dentist who is safe to call. They will not compare procedures
   until they feel safe.
2. **Multi-generational families.** Families measured in decades, children
   who grew up in the chair, booking across generations. Their job: keep
   everyone with one practice they already trust. This tenure is proof no
   competitor can copy.
3. **Cosmetic considerers.** Adults weighing veneers, whitening, or smile
   design. Their job: understand what a change involves before anything
   becomes permanent. Skeptical of upsell.

Context: mostly local mobile searches near an appointment moment. Phone is a
first-class conversion path, not a fallback.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Southern Dental Arts, a solo, founder-led family and
cosmetic dental practice in Raleigh, NC (Dr. Ryan K. Staehling, DDS).
Full redesign; the incumbent site is anti-reference, not style authority
(see `ANTI-REFERENCE.md`).

The site's job: convince an anxious or skeptical visitor that this practice
is safe to call, then route the two buyer types (family vs. cosmetic) into a
booking. Success is booked visits and calls, with the anxiety-handling
reputation (the practice's strongest reviewed trait) doing the
differentiating work.

## Brand Personality

Plain, warm, unhurried. Explains rather than sells. Confident without
superlatives.

Feeling: calm, human. A practice that has been in the same suite for years
and knows its patients' names. "Southern" reads as hospitality expressed
structurally (generous spacing, soft contrast, human photography, plain
language), never decoratively (no script fonts, no magnolia motifs, no
literal Southern iconography).

Tone references, structure and voice only, not visual copying:
grandstreetdental.com, atlantadentalspa.com.

Banned language (every Raleigh competitor already uses these): gentle,
compassionate, state-of-the-art, caring team, beautiful smiles, your smile
is our priority, dental family, cutting-edge, world-class, first-class,
patient-focused.

## Anti-references

Primary anti-reference: the current southerndentalarts.com.

- Five-slide auto-advancing hero carousel (the single worst element). No
  carousels anywhere on the new site.
- Flat 16-item service list with no hierarchy; three- and four-column
  symmetric card rows as the default section shape.
- Cool WordPress grays under warm brand hues (the core color failure).
- Stock dental photography: model patients, model dentists, uncanny smiles.
  No stock imagery, ever.
- Doctor bio in third-person marketing voice; Title Case On Every Heading.
- Social icons in the header, unlabeled form fields, primary CTA pointing at
  a staging domain.

Category anti-references: clinical-corporate dental chains and
premium-cosmetic-spa luxury. This site is not clinical, sterile, high-tech,
luxury, trendy, or startup.

## Design Principles

1. **Safety before selection.** An anxious visitor will not shop procedures
   until they feel safe. Every surface resolves anxiety before it asks for a
   decision; the anxiety answer always outranks the service catalog.
2. **Warmth is structural.** Hospitality is carried by pacing, spacing, soft
   contrast, plain language, and real photography. Never by ornament,
   scripts, or regional cliche.
3. **Real over plausible.** Real people, real rooms, real words, real
   numbers. If the real thing does not exist yet (photography, the doctor's
   own words, insurance data), the section waits or is omitted, visibly
   flagged. Never papered over with plausible fiction.
4. **Explain, don't sell.** State the conservative treatment philosophy as
   the practice's own, never as a comparison or a swipe at other practices.
   Tell people what can wait. No superlatives, no urgency mechanics.
5. **Two buyers, one practice.** Route family and cosmetic visitors early
   and clearly, but keep one voice: the cosmetic side never drifts
   spa-luxury, the family side never drifts clinical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, plus comfort accommodations for an audience that skews older
and anxious:

- Body text at 18px or larger.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation.
- No hover-only navigation; every panel also opens on click and Enter.
- Visible focus states everywhere; no suppressed outlines.
- Text-weight gold is always the dark gold token; decorative gold never
  carries text. Headings are olive on the warm base. Verify all contrast
  ratios in the built theme rather than trusting estimates (open item in
  `ANTI-REFERENCE.md`).
